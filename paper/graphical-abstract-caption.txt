Locally hosted language models benchmarked on journal editorial work, with their contribution separated from that of a deterministic checker of regular expressions and arithmetic. Left: manuscripts under review are confidential, so where policy forbids an external service the model must run on hardware the journal controls. Centre: the grid marks the 313 measured conditions, twenty open-weight models across eight editorial tasks, with different models eligible for each. Right: the bar spans the forty guideline violations seeded into a manuscript; the teal segment is what the checker recovers unaided, and the gold segment is what the strongest model adds.
